14.1. Pointed Models, Frame Conditions
Material in Section 7.2. applies to predicate logic without essential changes. In
particular, pointed frames and pointed models are defined exactly as before.
where < W,R,D > is a frame, G E W and RGw for all w E W. A pointed
model is a tuple M =< G,W,R,D,V > where < G,W,R,D > is a pointed
frame and V is a valuation on < W,R, D>. Truth in M is truth in the world

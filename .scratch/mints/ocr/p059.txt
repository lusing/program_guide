LEMMA 8.1. Every rule of LJpm has a subformula property: Each formula in
a premise is a subformula of some formula in the conclusion. Every derivation
consists of subformulas of its last sequent.
Proof.Check all rules.
Note that the cut rule does not have the subformula property. The systems
NJp and LJpm are equivalent; moreover their rules are in exact correspondence.

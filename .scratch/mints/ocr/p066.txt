8.4. Algebraic Models
Algebraic models represent another kind of model for non-classical logics. Such
a model usually consists of a universe U with a distinguished element T (truth)
and operations on U corresponding to logical operations. Conditions for op-
erations on U are set so that the validity of derivable formulas can be proved
rather easily. We show here that intuitionistic prepositional logic is sound and
complete for algebraic models.

Predicate logic terms are constructed from individuum variables denoted by
x, y, z, i,yi, z1 and so on, by means of function symbols denoted by f, g, h,
fi,g1,h1 and so on. Variables and O-place function symbols (constants) are
terms, and ifti,...,tp, (p > O) are terms, then f(ti,...,tp) is a term for a
p-place functional symbol f. Atomic formulas are constructed from terms and
predicate symbols (which are denoted by P, Q, Pi, Qi,... ). Propositional letters
are atomic formulas (and O-place predicate symbols); I is an atomic formula,
and if ti,...,tp, p > 0 are terms, then P(ti, ...,tp) is an atomic formula for a
p-place predicate symbol P. Formulas are constructed from atomic formulas by
propositional connectives &,V,→ and quantifiers. Atomic formulas are formu-
las, and if α, β are formulas, then α&β,α V β,α → β,Vrα, 3rα are formulas.

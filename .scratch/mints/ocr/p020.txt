2.5. Derivable Rules
It is often convenient to treat a series of inference rules as one rule.
DEFINITION 2.1. A deduction of a sequent S from sequents Si,..., Sn is a
tree beginning with axioms or sequents Si,:,:, Sn and proceeding by inference
rules.
A rule
St,..., Sn
is derivable if there is a deduction of S from Si,..:, Sn.

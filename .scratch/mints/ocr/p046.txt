6.1.
Structure of Normal Deduction
An occurrence of a subformula is positive in a formula if it is in the premise
of an even number (maybe O) of occurrences of implication. An occurrence is
strictly positive if it is not in the premise of any implication. An occurrence is
negative if it is not positive, that is, it is inside an odd number of premises of
implication. The sign of an occurrence in a sequent  =→ α is the same as in the
formula &r -→ α.

THEOREM 13.3. (Curry-Howard isomorphism between terms and natural
deductions in predicate logic)
(a) Every natural deduction d in NJ uniquely defines T(d) and vice versa: Every
deductive term t uniquely defines a natural deduction D(t).
(b) Cuts in d uniquely correspond to redexes in T(d) and vice versa.

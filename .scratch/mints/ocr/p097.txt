Godel's Negative Translation
Godel's negative translation still works for predicate logic, but Glivenko's theo-
rem (Theorem 3) does not extend literally: Formula Vα--Pα -→ --Vr Pα (and
intuitionistically equivalent -(Va--Pr&-Vα Pr)) are derivable in NK, but not
in NJ (see Example 14 below). Definition 3 is extended to quantifiers in a
natural way: V is preserved, 3 is prefixed by --.

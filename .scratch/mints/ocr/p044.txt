5.4. Consequences of Normalization
The principal formula of an elimination rule is the succedent formula explicitly
shown in the rule and containing eliminated connective: α&β in &E, and so on.
The principal premise contains the principal formula.
A main branch of a deduction is a branch ending in the final sequent and
containing principal premises of elimination rules with conclusions in the main
branch. Hence the main branch of a deduction ending in an introduction rule
branch up to the lowermost introduction rule or axiom.

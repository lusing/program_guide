THEOREM 13.4. (normalization theorem). (a) Every deductive term t can be
normalized.
(b) Every natural deduction d in NJ can be normalized.
Basic properties of normal deductions (Theorem 5.4.) are preserved, too,
and hence V-property and Harrop's theorem are extended to NJ together with
their proofs. In addition we have the 3-property.
